# LegoraBot Privacy Policy

Author: Stefano Puddu. Last updated: 22 October 2026.

LegoraBot is a Reddit Developer Platform (Devvit) app that helps moderate the r/Legora community. It is an unofficial community tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Legora.

**What data the app processes**

- Public post data on the subreddit where it is installed (post id, title, author username), used only to post a one-time welcome comment on a user's first post.
- A record of which usernames have already received a welcome comment, stored in the app's Reddit-hosted storage.
- Draft posts written by moderators or generated from public news headlines.

**What the app does not do**

- It does not collect personal data outside Reddit, sell data, track users across communities, or send private messages to users.
- External requests are limited to public RSS feeds of legal technology publications, to read public headlines. No Reddit user data is sent to these sites.

**Storage and deletion**

Data is stored using Reddit's Devvit storage and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. Questions: contact the moderators of r/Legora via modmail.
